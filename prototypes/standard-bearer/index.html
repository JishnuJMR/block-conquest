    THE FIELD GUIDE

    A little ground.
    A lot to play for.

    Hold more territory when the block countdown ends. Equal scores mean a draw. Both players may act during the match; there is no turn order.

    1. Claim. Any empty tile becomes yours with 1 power.
    2. Build. Reinforce your tile to add 1 power, up to the cap.
    3. Capture. Attack a weaker enemy directly above, below, left or right. Your source pays their power. The captured tile starts at 1.
    This study uses a short mock pending state before applying local changes. No wallet or transaction is involved. The scene selector below the field includes an opening board, attack preview, victory and draw. The mock clock starts paused.
