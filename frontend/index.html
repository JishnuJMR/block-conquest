    04 / FIELD GUIDE

    How conquest works

    1. 01
      Claim territory

      Choose any empty tile. It becomes yours with 1 power.

    2. 02
      Build power

      Reinforce when ready. The board sets its power cap and cooldown.

    3. 03
      Capture a weaker neighbour

      Attack up, down, left or right. Your power must be greater. Your source pays the defender’s power; your new tile starts at 1.

    Power decays; ownership stays. Every move needs a MetaMask confirmation and test ETH for gas.
